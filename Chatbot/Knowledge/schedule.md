# Event Schedule

The 25th-year biannual reunion spans two days, featuring events across the main campus. 

* **Day 1: Morning:** Inaugural ceremony and alumni keynote addresses at the main campus.
* **Day 1: Evening:** Cultural programs and live music bands at the JU Open Air Theatre.
* **Day 2: Morning:** Networking sessions, tech-talks, and department walkthroughs.
* **Day 2: Evening:** Grand Silver Jubilee Dinner.

For organizing queries or student coordination, see [Contact Information](./contact.md).
