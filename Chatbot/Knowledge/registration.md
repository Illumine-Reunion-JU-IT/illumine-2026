# Registration Guidelines

All alumni and current students must register to attend the 25th Biannual Reunion. 

**Steps to Register:**
1. Visit the official student-alumni portal at [illumine-ju-it.in](https://illumine-ju-it.in/).
2. Navigate to the "25th Reunion Registration" portal.
3. Fill in your graduation year or current academic year.
4. Pay the registration fee (discounted for current B.E. Information Technology students).

Once registered, you will receive an entry pass via email.
