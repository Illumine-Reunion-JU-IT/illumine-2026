# JU IT 25th Biannual Reunion

The Department of Information Technology at Jadavpur University is celebrating its 25th anniversary (Silver Jubilee). The biannual reunion brings together alumni, professors, and current students for networking, celebrations, and knowledge sharing. 

The event is closely associated with the department's traditions highlighted on [Illumine](https://illumine-ju-it.in/).
